Privacy & cookies

This page describes what the Codevalanche product website stores and what optional analytics does.

Essential storage and your choice

The first-party codevalanche_consent cookie remembers your explicit website analytics choice for 180 days. Loading a page does not renew it. The codevalanche_route_checked cookie prevents repeated country routing for one year, and codevalanche_language remembers the language selected in the footer for account pages. These essential preferences do not enable analytics. Theme preferences remain in codevalanche_theme. Essential account and security storage works with any analytics choice.

Optional website analytics

Website capture is currently disabled pending collector, provider and release qualification. No Google Analytics is loaded. Once enabled, our first-party SDK sends observations only after your acceptance and a valid consent receipt. The recipient is Radovar, operating Codevalanche, with qualified Supabase infrastructure as a processor. No external analytics dashboard or third-party tracker is required.

Optional observations include registered pages, section reach, estimated visible and active time, scroll buckets, feature and FAQ choices, account entry intent, download clicks and supported browser performance measurements. They do not prove reading, completed downloads or installation. Account activity has a separate signed-in choice, verified against the current account and consent state. Website acceptance cannot enable it. Public optional capture remains disabled for this release.

Only allowlisted campaign parameters (source, medium, campaign, content and term) and the normalized external referrer domain may be used after consent. Unsafe values are rejected. Missing or same-origin referrers remain unknown. We send coarse device, operating-system and browser families, viewport buckets and locale, never the raw user agent, exact screen size or a country inferred by an external lookup. Raw URLs, queries, fragments, advertising click IDs, form values, email addresses, prompts, source code, project paths and credentials are excluded.

Identifiers, retention and withdrawal

After consent and receipt validation, an anonymous browser ID lasts up to 30 days; a session lasts up to 24 hours with a 30-minute idle limit. Website IDs and queues use first-party owned_analytics:codevalanche storage. Queues are bounded to 100 observations younger than 24 hours. Delivery is best effort and can fail on navigation, process loss or unavailable storage. Raw analytics retention is 90 days; non-identifying aggregates are retained for 13 months when the qualified service is activated.

Manage cookies is always available on these pages. Declining or withdrawing immediately stops optional emission and clears optional IDs and queued observations. An offline revocation retains only the receipt revocation proof until acknowledgement. Withdrawal does not retroactively erase accepted observations; deletion requests follow the separate privacy process. Website history is never merged into a later account.

Account activity and security

Account activity analytics requires a separate explicit choice in your signed-in account. Website analytics acceptance does not enable account attribution. Events include an opaque event and installation ID, verified account ID, platform, app version and timestamps. Activity such as app openings or remote reconnects is separated from confirmed provider or execution receipts. Client reports alone do not prove successful runs. No prompts, source code, file paths, passwords, tokens or recovery codes are included.

Withdrawing account analytics consent stops new collection and rejects queued events from an earlier consent choice. Guest activity is not automatically attached to an account, and anonymous history is not merged into account activity. Optional website-account observations use a separate receipt and can include profile-page views, profile-edit and save intentions, logout clicks and estimated active time; profile values and credentials are excluded. Intentions are separate from confirmed operational outcomes.

Application-managed account analytics and security events use a 90-day retention policy; complete IP addresses, if collected by a qualified source, are removed within 7 days. Administrative audit uses a 365-day policy. Provider Auth, delivery and ingress records have separate retention settings. Deleting application records does not promise deletion of provider copies or local projects.

Canonical display profiles and profile-save idempotency keys are functional account records retained until account erasure, separately from 90-day raw operational outcomes. Successful retries can replace an earlier terminal profile failure; detailed terminal profile outcomes are not promised beyond the raw window.

Download clicks, gateway redirects and provider counter changes are different observations, not completed transfers, installations or activations. Unqualified release, delivery and native sources remain unavailable for measurement. Hosting and infrastructure providers may retain delivery/security logs including network metadata under separate settings; the owned analytics retention does not establish those providers’ retention.

You can review permitted security activity in your account. Provider coverage and delayed sources are shown explicitly; missing data is not treated as zero attempts. Security audit is required to protect account access and remains separate from optional product analytics.

Your choice

You can decline analytics, or change your preference at any time using Manage cookies in the account page or homepage footer. Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser also removes the stored preference.
