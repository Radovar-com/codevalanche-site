Privacy & cookies

This page describes what the Codevalanche product website stores and what optional analytics does.

Essential storage

The site stores a first-party cookie named codevalanche_consent to remember whether you accepted or declined optional analytics, and a browser localStorage entry named codevalanche_theme to remember your light or dark theme choice. Neither leaves your browser. It is used only for that preference and expires after one year.

Optional analytics

If you choose Accept analytics, the site may load Google Analytics 4 using the measurement ID configured for this website. It is used for aggregate page views and basic interactions such as section navigation and feature selection. It is not used to receive source code, prompts, form data, account information, query-string contents, or browsing history from this site.

If the website has not been configured with a real measurement ID, no analytics provider is loaded even after acceptance.

First-party product analytics

After you explicitly accept analytics, a configured Codevalanche service may receive website visits and download clicks. These events contain a random event ID, a random browser installation ID, the event name, the platform and your analytics consent. They do not contain page URLs, query strings, form values, account details or source code. A browser installation ID is stored in localStorage as codevalanche_analytics_installation only after acceptance. Withdrawing consent stops collection and removes that identifier. This does not delete events already received by the service.

If no analytics service origin is configured, no first-party events are sent. Website analytics does not establish that a download completed or that the app was installed.

Account activity and security

Account activity analytics requires a separate explicit choice in your signed-in account. Website analytics acceptance does not enable account attribution. Events include an opaque event and installation ID, verified account ID, platform, app version and timestamps. Activity such as app openings or remote reconnects is separated from confirmed provider or execution receipts. Client reports alone do not prove successful runs. No prompts, source code, file paths, passwords, tokens or recovery codes are included.

Withdrawing account analytics consent stops new collection and rejects queued events from an earlier consent choice. Guest activity is not automatically attached to an account, and anonymous history is not merged into account activity.

Application-managed account analytics and security events use a 90-day retention policy; complete IP addresses, if collected by a qualified source, are removed within 7 days. Administrative audit uses a 365-day policy. Provider Auth, delivery and ingress records have separate retention settings. Deleting application records does not promise deletion of provider copies or local projects.

You can review permitted security activity in your account. Provider coverage and delayed sources are shown explicitly; missing data is not treated as zero attempts. Security audit is required to protect account access and remains separate from optional product analytics.

Your choice

You can decline analytics, or change your preference at any time using Manage cookies in the account page or homepage footer. Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser also removes the stored preference.
