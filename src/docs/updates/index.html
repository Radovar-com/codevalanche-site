    Steps

    1. After an update, Codevalanche shows a short card with what changed, so no improvement passes you by.

    2. It appears once per version and stays closed once you dismiss it. The full list of changes is always in Settings, under About.

    3. Checking for updates now tells you plainly when no new version has been published yet.

    4. Updates are signed and checked before they install. Feedback you send from the app leaves without your keys or personal paths.
