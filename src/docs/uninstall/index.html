    Steps

    1. Back up the work you want to keep, close the app, then uninstall Codevalanche through Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps.

    2. Your conversations and run history, preferences, Computer Use rules, voice transcripts and the app log (rotated, about 100 MB at most, with passwords and tokens masked). API keys, connector sign-ins and your account session are stored encrypted, tied to your Windows account. Your account session works offline for at most 30 days, then the app asks you to sign in again. You can delete all of this from the app or by uninstalling it and removing its folder. The AI tools themselves (Claude Code, Codex and others) keep their own data in their own folders.
