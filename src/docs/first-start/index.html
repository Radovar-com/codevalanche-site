    Steps

    1. Do I need a Codevalanche account?

      No. You can use the app as a guest. A Codevalanche account lets you sign in on the desktop app and manage your profile and sessions on this website.

    2. Sign in to Claude Code or Codex with your own account, or choose a local model that runs on your PC.

    3. Open the repository that is already on your PC, with its files, folders and version control. Nothing to import or rebuild.

    4. Use a disposable project or a repository with a clean starting point. Check the selected account, model and permission mode.
